About

Photographs with a sense of place

I am Alex Rivera, a photographer based in the Pacific Northwest. For over a decade I have chased quiet light across coastlines, cities and quiet rooms, making images that feel honest and unhurried.

My work has appeared in independent magazines and private collections. I shoot on both digital and film, and hand-finish every print.
