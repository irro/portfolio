About

Hello, I’m Alex Rivera

A landscape and portrait photographer chasing honest light across the Pacific Northwest and beyond.

I picked up my first camera fifteen years ago and never really put it down. What began as a way to remember places became a practice of paying attention — to weather, to faces, to the small moments that pass when no one is looking.

Today I split my time between commissioned editorial work and a long-running personal series on changing coastlines. I shoot on both digital and medium-format film, and I hand-finish every print that leaves the studio.

Selected exhibitions & clients

  • Editorial commissions for independent travel magazines

How I work
